## 📊 ATPS Sales & Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Sales & Analytics Dashboard** designed to provide a comprehensive overview of sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, and regional trends.

### 🔍 Key Features
- **Net Sales by Period & Segment** – Analyze sales trends across different periods and customer segments.
- **Sales by Segment** – Compare performance across Government, Small Business, Enterprise, Midmarket, and Channel Partners.
- **Units Sold by Segment** – Track total units sold and segment-wise contribution.
- **Sales & Returns Analysis** – Monitor monthly sales and returns percentages.
- **Sales by Category** – Compare sales performance across product categories.
- **Spend by Day** – Analyze daily spending patterns and identify sales trends over time.
- **Detailed Sales Table** – Provides transaction-level information including Gross Sales, Discounts, Net Sales, COGS, Profit, Profit Margin, Manufacturing Price, and Units Sold.
- **Dynamic Filters** – Dashboard values update based on selected filters and the latest available data refresh.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- **Data Visualization**
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**
- **Business Intelligence**

### 🎯 Objective
The objective of this dashboard is to transform raw sales data into **actionable business insights**, helping stakeholders understand sales performance, profitability, customer-segment contribution, and overall business trends.
